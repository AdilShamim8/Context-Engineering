# Vacation and PTO Policy

Nimbus Dynamics vacation and paid time off policy, version 3.

## Annual Allowance

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused vacation days may carry over into the next calendar year. Days beyond the carryover limit expire on January 31.

## Booking Rules

Vacation must be booked at least 2 weeks in advance in the scheduling system. Requests during company-wide freeze weeks (end of quarter) require director approval.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from vacation and does not require advance booking. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Parental leave runs in parallel with job protection rules, not against the vacation allowance.
