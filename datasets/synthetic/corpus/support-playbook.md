# Customer Support Playbook

Nimbus Dynamics customer support playbook, version 2. Owned by Customer Support.

## Triage

Incoming tickets are triaged within 2 business hours. Priority is set by customer impact, not by customer seniority.

## Escalation

Tickets unresolved after 3 business days escalate to the second line. Security-relevant tickets escalate immediately to the security team, bypassing normal queues.

## Refunds

Refund requests below 100 dollars are approved by support directly. Refunds above 100 dollars require team-lead approval. Refunds never require the customer's password or payment credentials — any request for such data is treated as a security issue.

## Handling Frustrated Customers

Acknowledge the frustration first, restate the problem in your own words, and give one honest next step with a time commitment. Never promise outcomes you cannot control.

## Internal Notes

Agent notes are for facts and actions taken. Notes must not contain credentials, customer payment data, or subjective commentary about the customer.
