# Remote Work Policy

Nimbus Dynamics remote work policy, version 2. This policy applies to all full-time employees in every office.

## Remote Days

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. The remaining days are expected on-site for collaboration, with exceptions granted by department heads for medical or family needs.

## Approval Process

Remote work requests must be approved by the direct manager at least 48 hours in advance. Managers respond within one business day.

## Cross-Time-Zone Work

Employees working from a different time zone for more than two consecutive weeks must file a temporary relocation notice with People Ops for compliance purposes.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and one monitor for remote work. Additional equipment requests go through the standard IT procurement process.
