# Data Retention Schedule

Nimbus Dynamics data retention schedule, version 2. Owned by Legal and Compliance.

## General Records

Business correspondence is retained for 3 years. Meeting notes are retained for 2 years and then archived cold.

## Support Tickets

Resolved customer support tickets are retained for 18 months. Tickets linked to contractual obligations follow the contract term plus 2 years.

## Chat History

Internal chat history is retained for 90 days by default. Retention exceptions for legal matters are set by the compliance team only.

## Application Logs

Application logs are retained for 30 days. Security-relevant logs are retained for 1 year and access to them is restricted to the security team.

## Deletion Requests

Employee data deletion requests are processed within 30 calendar days, subject to statutory retention obligations that take precedence over this schedule.
