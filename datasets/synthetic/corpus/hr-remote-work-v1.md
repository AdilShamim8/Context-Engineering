# Remote Work Policy

Nimbus Dynamics remote work policy, version 1. **This version was superseded by version 2, effective 2026-08-14. It is kept in the corpus only for versioning and conflict-detection labs.**

## Remote Days

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. There is no formal approval requirement for the standard arrangement.

## Approval Process

Remote work arrangements are recorded by the employee's manager but do not require advance approval.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop. Monitors are the employee's responsibility.
