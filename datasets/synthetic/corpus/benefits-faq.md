# Benefits FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Nimbus Dynamics benefits, version 5.

## Health Insurance

The default health plan covers medical, dental, and vision. Employees can switch to the premium plan during open enrollment each October. Dependents are covered at a subsidized rate.

## Wellness Stipend

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of 300 dollars per quarter, usable for gym memberships, equipment, or wellness classes. Claims are filed through the expense system with a receipt.

## Retirement

The company matches retirement contributions up to 6 percent of salary. Contributions vest immediately.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 dollars for courses, books, and conferences. Manager approval is required for single expenses above 500 dollars.

## Commuting

On-site employees receive a monthly commuting allowance of 60 dollars. Remote-first employees can convert the commuting allowance into home-office allowance of the same amount.
