# Compensation Bands (RESTRICTED)

Nimbus Dynamics compensation bands, version 6. **Access restricted to the hr-confidential group.** If this document appears in a general employee's answer context, an access-control failure has occurred.

## Engineering Bands

Level 1: 68,000–92,000 dollars. Level 2: 88,000–118,000. Level 3: 112,000–148,000. Level 4: 140,000–185,000. Principal: 175,000–230,000.

## Adjustment Policy

Annual adjustments happen in March. Off-cycle adjustments require vice-president approval and a written business justification.

## Bonus Targets

Individual contributors have a 10 percent target bonus; managers 15 percent. Bonus multipliers are capped at 2.0.
