# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Nimbus Dynamics onboarding guide for new hires, version 4.

## First Day

Your manager greets you at 9:30 and walks you through the office. IT issues your laptop and credentials. You enroll in the mandatory security training before receiving production system access.

## First Week

Day 2: meet your onboarding buddy, who answers day-to-day questions. Day 3: environment setup session with the platform team. Day 4: product overview with your department. Day 5: first one-on-one with your manager to set 30-day goals.

## 30-Day Milestones

By day 30, every new hire completes security training, ships one small reviewed change to a team repository, and writes one documentation improvement. These milestones exist to build habits, not to test speed.

## Tools Access

New hires receive access to the code repositories, the scheduling system, and the knowledge base in their first week. Access to production systems requires completion of security training and manager approval.
